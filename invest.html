Seed Investment種子投資

I invest in people with ideas.我投資有想法嘅年輕人。

Seen too many ups and downs to be impressed by talk. If you have ideas, guts and the willingness to work, I am willing to sit down. Not just money — 26 years of experience and connections come with it.見過太多起落，知道咩係真嘅咩係吹水。如果你有想法、有膽識、肯做，我願意傾。唔係淨係錢，仲有 26 年嘅經驗同人脈。

What I look for我睇三樣嘢

💡 Ideas有想法

You have spotted a problem nobody has solved, and you have your own answer. It does not have to be high tech — getting one step of a traditional trade right is a business too.你見到一個別人未解決嘅問題，而你有自己嘅答案。唔一定要高科技——傳統行業嘅一個環節做好，都可以係一門生意。

🎯 Guts有膽識

You are willing to carry the risk of your own judgement. Business always has uncertainty; the question is whether you will bet on your own read.願意為自己嘅判斷承擔風險。做生意一定有唔確定嘅時候，問題係你敢唔敢在自己嘅判斷上落注。

🔨 Willingness to work肯做

You start from the smallest task and are not afraid to get your hands dirty. I have seen too many good talkers who never chased the first order or the first customer.由最細嘅事做起，唔怕落手落腳。我睇過太多人講得漂亮，但連第一批貨、第一個客都未去追過。

What comes with it我帶嚟乜

💰 Capital資金

Seed-stage money to get started. The amount depends on the project and we will be clear about it when we talk.種子階段嘅啟動資金。金額視項目而定，談嘅時候會講清楚。

🧭 Experience經驗

26 years across 7 industries: electronics, garments, jewellery, e-bikes, training, cross-border e-commerce, AI compute. You do not have to step in the same holes I did.26 年、7 個行業：電子家電、服裝鞋履、珠寶電車、教育培訓、跨境電商、AI 算力。踩過嘅坑，你可以唔使再踩一次。

🤝 Connections人脈

500+ clients served. The relationships — factories, logistics, platforms, overseas buyers — are still there.服務過 500+ 客戶，工廠、物流、平台、海外買家嘅關係都仲在。

🗣️ Straight talk直話

I will not tell you your idea is great just because I want in. If it is not worth doing, I will say so.唔會因為想投你而話你個項目好。唔值得做嘅，我會直接講。

How to reach me點樣搵我

Write to me in one paragraph: what problem you are solving, who pays for it, and what you have already done. A rough one-pager beats a polished deck.用一段話講清楚：你解決緊乜問題、邊個會俾錢、你已經做咗啲乜。一頁講得清嘅草稿，好過一百頁靚 deck。
