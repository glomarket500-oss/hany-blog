For studios: how to go live給工作室：怎樣開直播

Five things, none of them technical. If you can make a case, you can do this.五樣嘢，冇一樣係技術問題。你做到一個殼，就做到呢件事。

1. A phone will do一、手機就夠

The rear camera on any phone made in the last five years is better than the webcam we would buy you. Shoot landscape so the bench fits the frame.近五年出嘅任何一部手機，後鏡都好過我們買畀你嘅 webcam。橫向拍，先裝得落整張工作檯。

2. Something to hold it二、要有個架

A tripod, a clamp or a shelf above the bench. Anything that stops the camera moving when you work. This matters more than camera quality.三腳架、夾具，或者工作檯上方嘅層板。只要令部機喺你工作時唔會郁。呢樣比鏡頭質素重要。

3. Light on the work三、光要照住工件

Put the light between the camera and your hands, never behind the work. One window or one lamp is enough. Shadows across the piece are what make a stream unwatchable.把光放在鏡頭同你雙手之間，唔好放喺工件後面。一個窗或者一盞燈就夠。工件上有陰影，就係直播最難睇嘅原因。

4. Internet that holds四、網絡要穩

A stable connection matters more than a fast one. Test a five-minute private stream before you go public; if it drops, lower the quality rather than moving the bench.穩定比快重要。公開之前，先做一次五分鐘嘅私人測試直播；如果斷線，降低畫質，唔好搬張檯。

5. One account, one channel五、一個帳號，一條頻道

A YouTube account in the studio's name, with the country in the channel title. Send us the channel URL and we add it to your slot. No password ever changes hands.用工作室名義開一個 YouTube 帳號，頻道名要有國家。把頻道網址發給我們，我們就接入你嘅格。任何情況下都唔需要交出密碼。

What we ask in return我們要求嘅回報

Stream the work, not the sales pitch. No prices on camera, no customer names, no other brand's products in frame. A stream that turns into an advertisement is worse than no stream at all.拍工序，唔好拍推銷。鏡頭前唔講價、唔提客人名、唔出其他品牌嘅產品。一場變成廣告嘅直播，比冇直播更差。

This list is written for a craftsman with a phone, not for an engineer with a studio. None of the studios on the roadmap has opened, so none of them has been sent it yet.呢張清單係寫畀一個得部手機嘅師傅，唔係寫畀一個有晒器材嘅工程師。路線圖上嘅工作室一間都未開，所以暫時一間都未收到。
