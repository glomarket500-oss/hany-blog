Studio setup costs, timelines and materials routes工作室開辦成本、時間表同材料路線

Every node in the network, with the cost line items behind the figure. The three prototype budgets are from our own feasibility work — a Maître studio at US$60,000, an assembly node at US$57,000 and an African node at US$34,300 — and the ranges around them are derived from local rent, wage and equipment data, labelled as estimates with their basis stated.網絡上每一個節點，連數字背後嘅成本分項。三個原型預算來自我們自己嘅可行性研究——名匠工作室 US$60,000、組裝節點 US$57,000、非洲節點 US$34,300——而周圍嘅範圍係按當地租金、工資同設備數據推算，並標明為估算，附推算依據。

How to read these figures. Where a cost came from a published source — Bangladesh's own technology stock-take lists leather machinery sets at about US$2,000–2,500 and US$6,200–6,400 FOB; Vietnam's regional minimum wage rose 7.2% on 1 January 2026 to VND 5.31m (about US$210 a month) in Region 1 — the source is named in the line. Everything else is a derived range from local commercial rent, wage and equipment data, and is labelled as such. No figure here is a quotation.點睇呢批數字。有公開來源嘅，就在分項裡寫明出處——孟加拉官方皮革業技術清單列出皮革機械組合約 US$2,000–2,500 及 US$6,200–6,400 FOB；越南一區最低工資已於 2026 年 1 月 1 日上調 7.2% 至 531 萬越南盾（約每月 US$210）。其餘都係由當地商用租金、工資同設備數據推算嘅範圍，並已標明。本頁冇任何數字係報價。
