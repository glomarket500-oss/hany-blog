Two honest limits. First, deadlines: the dispatch times are what our ateliers are contracted to, and a hand-made piece can slip by a few days; if it does, we tell you before the date passes rather than after. Second, colours: full-grain hide is not a paint, so a Tan case may arrive a shade darker or lighter than the photograph, and that variation is the material, not a fault.兩個老實嘅限制。第一，交期：出貨時間係我們同工坊約定嘅，而手作品有時會遲幾日；真係遲，我們會在到期之前通知你，唔會過期才講。第二，顏色：頭層皮唔係油漆，所以原色棕嘅殼可能比相片深或者淺一個色階——呢個差異係材料本身，唔係瑕疵。
