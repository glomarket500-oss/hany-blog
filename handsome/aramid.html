Aramid Fiber芳綸纖維

Aramid fiber is the same family of material used in bullet-resistant vests and racing composites — which does not make a phone case bulletproof. In a case it is used for a different reason: it is the only common case material that can be thin, light and stiff at the same time.芳綸纖維與防彈背心、賽車複合材料同屬一個材料家族——但這並不代表手機殼能防彈。用在手機殼上，理由不同：它是常見殼料中唯一能同時做到薄、輕、硬的。
