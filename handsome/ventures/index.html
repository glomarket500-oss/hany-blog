How I choose怎么选项目

Three questions before a new brand.开新品牌前的三个问题。

01

Can it be made properly at a small scale?小规模也能做好吗？

If quality only works at mass volume, it is not the right first product for me.如果只有大规模才能保证质量，那它不适合做我的第一个产品。

02

Does it have a real reason to exist?它存在的理由真实吗？

A cheaper version of an existing product is not a reason.把现有产品做得更便宜，不算理由。

03

Can it be sold outside China?能卖到中国以外吗？

Specs, compliance, packaging and support have to work for export from the start.规格、合规、包装和售后，从第一天就要按出口来做。

Building something adjacent?在做相关的事？

If you make materials, packaging or retail, I'd like to hear from you.如果你做材料、包装或零售，欢迎联系。
