Aramid fiber芳綸纖維

0.65–0.75 mm · 11–13 g0.65–0.75 毫米 · 11–13 克

The only common case material that stays thin, light and rigid at the same time. The weave you can feel is the material itself, not a printed pattern.常見殼料中唯一能同時做到薄、輕、硬嘅一種。你摸到嘅織紋就係材料本身，唔係印刷圖案。

Where it fails:邊度會失手： rigid is not the same as shock-absorbing. Aramid spreads an impact; it does not cushion one.硬唔等於吸震。芳綸係分散衝擊，唔係緩衝衝擊。

Full-grain leather頭層皮革

1.6–1.9 mm · 24–29 g1.6–1.9 毫米 · 24–29 克

The top layer of the hide with the grain intact. It darkens and softens, and it records where you have been. Nothing else in the range improves with use.皮料最外層，保留完整粒面。會變深、變軟，記錄你去過邊度。全線只有佢會越用越好。

Where it fails:邊度會失手： it needs conditioning every few months and it will scuff. A case that looks new forever is a case made of plastic.每幾個月要上一次保養膏，而且一定會刮花。想永遠似新，就只可以用塑膠。

Drop protection防摔結構

2.4–2.8 mm · 32–38 g2.4–2.8 毫米 · 32–38 克

Engineering rather than thickness: a rigid back panel spreads the impact, air-cushion corners give it somewhere to go, and raised lips keep the glass off the table.靠工程，唔係靠厚度：硬質背板分散衝擊、氣囊護角提供形變空間、加高邊緣讓玻璃唔碰桌面。

Where it fails:邊度會失手： it is the heaviest and thickest of the three, and MIL-STD-810G is a target here, not a certificate. Ask us for the lab name.三款中最重最厚。而且 MIL-STD-810G 係目標，唔係證書——你可以問我們實驗室嘅名。
