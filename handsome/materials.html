Materials & testing材質與測試

What each material actually is, and what the numbers behind a case claim mean.每一種材質究竟是什麼，以及手機殼那些宣稱背後的數字代表什麼。

1. Aramid fiber1. 芳綸纖維

Aramid is a synthetic fibre with very high tensile strength and low density. Woven into a sheet and laminated with resin, it makes a case shell that is roughly half the thickness of a TPU case at similar stiffness.芳綸是一種高拉伸強度、低密度的合成纖維。織成布再與樹脂複合，在相近剛性下，殼體厚度大約是 TPU 殼的一半。

Watch out: many cheap “aramid” cases are printed polycarbonate with a weave pattern. A real aramid case weighs 11–14 g and feels slightly cool and dry; a printed one usually weighs 25 g or more and shows a repeating printed texture under a light.注意：市面上很多便宜的「芳綸」殼其實是印了編織紋的 PC 殼。真的芳綸殼重約 11–14 克，摸起來微涼偏乾；印刷的通常 25 克以上，用光一照會看到重複的印刷紋路。

2. Leather2. 皮革

Full-grain is the outermost layer of the hide and keeps the natural grain. Top-grain is sanded smooth; bonded leather is scraps glued together. Only full-grain develops a real patina. We use full-grain, vegetable-tanned hides over a TPU frame.頭層皮是皮料最外層，保留天然粒面；二層皮經過打磨；再生皮是碎料黏合。只有頭層皮會形成真正的包漿。我們用頭層植鞣皮，內裡是 TPU 吸震框。

  • Full-grain頭層皮Keeps the grain, ages best保留粒面，養成效果最好
  • Top-grain二層皮Sanded smooth, more uniform, less patina打磨過，較均勻，包漿較弱
  • Bonded再生皮Glued scraps — avoid碎料黏合——建議避開

3. Drop protection3. 防摔

The usual reference is MIL-STD-810G, Method 516.6, Procedure IV: 26 drops from 1.2 m onto a hard surface, covering corners, edges and faces. A case that genuinely passes it usually has a stiff back panel, a flexible frame, and corner voids.常用參考是 MIL-STD-810G 方法 516.6 程序 IV：從 1.2 米高處跌落到硬質表面共 26 次，涵蓋角、邊、面。真正通過的殼通常有硬質背板、柔性邊框，以及角落的空腔結構。

4. Magnets and MagSafe4. 磁石與 MagSafe

MagSafe compatibility is a magnet array, not a logo. An N52 ring aligned to the coil holds accessories securely and lets charging through; a weak or misaligned ring does neither. Aramid and polycarbonate do not block the field — thick metal plates do.MagSafe 相容靠的是磁石陣列，不是一個標誌。對齊線圈的 N52 磁石環能牢固吸住配件並讓無線充電通過；磁力弱或位置偏移則兩者都做不到。芳綸與 PC 不會阻擋磁場，厚的金屬片才會。

How to judge any case, including ours怎樣判斷任何一個殼，包括我們的

Weigh it磅重量

If an “aramid” case weighs over 20 g, it is probably not aramid.「芳綸」殼如果超過 20 克，大概不是芳綸。

Measure it量厚度

A caliper settles most marketing claims in ten seconds.一把卡尺十秒鐘就能驗證大部分宣傳。

Check the lips看加高

Put it face-down on a table. If the glass touches, it is not protecting the screen.正面朝下放桌上。如果玻璃碰到桌面，它就沒在保護屏幕。

Ask for the test要測試報告

Any brand claiming MIL-STD-810G should be able to name the lab.任何聲稱通過 MIL-STD-810G 的品牌，都應該說得出是哪家實驗室。
