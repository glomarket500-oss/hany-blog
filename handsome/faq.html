Frequently asked常見問題

If your question is not here, email us — a real person answers.如果這裡沒有你的問題，直接 email，會有真人回覆。

Which one should I buy?我應該買哪一款？
If you want the thinnest case, take Aramid. If you want something that ages and feels warm, take Leather. If you have broken a screen before, take Shield.想要最薄選芳綸；想要會養成、手感溫潤選皮革；摔裂過屏幕就選防摔。
Are these real aramid fiber?這些是真的芳綸纖維嗎？
Yes. Aramid cases weigh 11–13 g and are 0.65–0.75 mm thick. You can verify both with a kitchen scale and a caliper.是。芳綸殼重 11–13 克、厚 0.65–0.75 毫米，你用廚房磅和卡尺就能自己驗證。
Do they work with wireless charging?支援無線充電嗎？
Yes. Aramid and polycarbonate do not block the magnetic field. All models ship with an N52 magnet array aligned to the charging coil.支援。芳綸與 PC 不會阻擋磁場。所有型號都內置對齊充電線圈的 N52 磁石環。
Will the leather case protect my phone?皮革殼能保護手機嗎？
It absorbs everyday knocks well because of the TPU frame, but it is not a rugged case. For drops onto concrete from height, choose Shield.因為有 TPU 內框，日常碰撞沒問題，但它不是防摔殼。從高處摔到水泥地，請選防摔系列。
Which phones do you support?支援哪些手機型號？
iPhone and Samsung Galaxy are the standard lines. Tell us your model when you order — if we do not have it, we will say so rather than take your money.標準線是 iPhone 與 Samsung Galaxy。下單時告知型號；如果沒有，我們會直接說，不會先收錢。
How do I order?怎樣下單？
Email us with your phone model and the finish you want. Online checkout is being built.把型號與想要的款式 email 給我們。網上結賬正在開發中。
How long does shipping take?運送要多久？
Usually 7–15 business days depending on destination. We confirm the estimate before you pay, and we tell you if we cannot ship to your country.視目的地而定，通常 7–15 個工作天。我們會在收款前確認時間；寄不到的地區會直接說明。
Can I return it?可以退貨嗎？
Yes — unused, in original packaging, within 30 days of delivery. See Shipping & Returns for the full terms.可以——未使用、原包裝，收貨後 30 天內。完整條款見「配送與退換」。
Do you make custom designs?可以做訂造嗎？
For bulk or corporate orders, yes. Email us with quantity and artwork.批量或企業訂單可以。請 email 數量和圖稿。
Why are your prices lower than big brands?為什麼價格比大牌低？
We do not spend on celebrity gifting or retail flagship stores. The material cost difference between our three collections is visible in the price, and that is the whole pricing logic.我們不花錢在明星送禮和旗艦店上。三個系列之間的價差就是材質成本的分別，定價邏輯就這麼簡單。
