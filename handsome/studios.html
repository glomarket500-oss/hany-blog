How a studio operates工作室怎樣運作

Five rules, the same in every country. They are the only reason a network like this can hold one standard.五條規矩，每個國家都一樣。呢個網絡之所以守得住一個標準，就靠呢五條。

Materials ship from Hong Kong材料由香港發運

Aramid, leather and hardware are bought centrally in the Pearl River Delta, inspected and graded once, then consolidated into a single shipment. One buyer and one grading standard is what stops twenty studios from drifting into twenty different products, and it is the cheapest part of the structure to get right. It also matters most where it is hardest: a workshop node that imports everything depends on it.芳綸、皮革同五金由珠三角統一採購，驗一次貨、分一次級，再拼成一個櫃發運。一個買手、一個分級標準，就係防止二十間工作室漂移成二十種產品嘅方法，亦係整個結構中最便宜嘅一環。而且愈係樣樣都要入口嘅工坊節點，愈要倚靠呢一環。

Craftsmen teach craftsmen工匠帶工匠

Every studio carries apprentices, and every core craftsman is expected to work in at least two countries. Technique is recorded on video rather than written down, because a saddle stitch and a burnished edge cannot be explained in a document. The acceptance standard is a number — thickness, stitch pitch, pull strength — not a house style.每間工作室都帶學徒，而每位核心師傅都要至少在兩個國家做過。技法用影片記錄，唔係寫落文件——馬鞍縫同燙邊，用文字講唔清。驗收標準係數字：厚度、縫距、拉力值，唔係邊個嘅風格。

The centre funds the studio中心出資開工作室

Hong Kong provides the opening capital, the first materials and the trade credit as a convertible loan, not a gift. Each studio keeps its own profit and loss from day one. The centre retains a quality veto and the right to audit, and says so before anyone signs.香港出開辦資金、首批材料同採購信用，形式係可轉換借款，唔係送。每間工作室由第一日起就自己一個損益表。中心保留品質否決權同查帳權，而且喺簽字之前就講明。

A young operator runs it年輕營運者主理

The person who runs a studio is chosen for judgement and integrity as much as skill, and is trained before the studio opens rather than after. One manager covers eight studios at most. That is a ceiling, not a target.主理一間工作室嘅人，揀嘅係判斷力同誠信，唔止手藝；而且係開店之前培訓，唔係開完再學。一位管理者最多管八間工作室。呢個係上限，唔係目標。

The founder stands behind it創辦人企喺後面

The founder is the backer and the elder, not the man on the bench. If a studio has to close, he explains it himself. Brand, capital and reputation all sit in Hong Kong, and that is the point of the structure.創辦人係出資人同長輩，唔係落手做嗰位。要收一間工作室嘅時候，由佢親自解釋。品牌、資金同信用都放在香港——呢個就係整個結構嘅意義。

The uncomfortable part唔舒服嘅部分

Hand-made studios cannot out-produce a factory, so atelier output alone caps this business at roughly US$5–6M. Volume belongs in a central workshop; the studios keep the work that cannot be standardised. Both halves are in the plan, and pretending otherwise would be the expensive mistake.手作工作室嘅效率永遠低過工廠，所以單靠名匠產能，呢門生意上限大約 US$5–6M。量產交給中央工坊，工作室只做無法標準化嘅部分。兩邊都在計劃之內——假裝只有一邊，就係最貴嘅錯誤。

This page describes an operating model, not an operating company. Studio statuses, city choices and every capacity field are placeholders to be replaced as real studios open. Today the count of operating studios is zero; Hong Kong is a centre, not a workshop.本頁講嘅係一套運作模式，唔係一間運作中嘅公司。工作室狀態、城市選擇同所有產能欄位都係佔位，會隨真實工作室開業而取代。今日實際運作嘅工作室數目係零；香港係中心，唔係工作室。
