How a studio operates工作室怎樣運作

Five rules, the same in every country. They are the only reason a network like this can hold one standard.五條規矩，每個國家都一樣。呢個網絡之所以守得住一個標準，就靠呢五條。

Read the five rules睇五條規矩

Materials ship from Hong Kong材料由香港發運 — bought centrally in the Pearl River Delta, inspected and graded once, then consolidated into a single shipment. Landed cost runs 5–12% above local sourcing and lead time 48–81 days — that is the honest price of one standard.由珠三角統一採購，驗一次貨、分一次級，再拼成一個櫃發運。落地成本比本地採購高 5–12%，交期 48–81 天——呢個就係「同一個標準」嘅真實代價。

Craftsmen teach craftsmen工匠帶工匠 — every studio carries apprentices, every core craftsman works in at least two countries, and technique is recorded on video because a saddle stitch cannot be explained in a document.每間工作室都帶學徒，每位核心師傅都要至少在兩個國家做過；技法用影片記錄——馬鞍縫用文字講唔清。

The centre funds the studio中心出資開工作室 — Hong Kong provides opening capital, first materials and trade credit as a convertible loan, not a gift: 6% a year, interest-only for 24 months, plus 8% of turnover for 36 months. Each studio keeps its own profit and loss from day one.香港出開辦資金、首批材料同採購信用，形式係可轉換借款，唔係送：年息 6%，首 24 個月只還息，另加首 36 個月營業額 8% 分成。每間工作室由第一日起就自己一個損益表。

A young operator runs it年輕營運者主理 — chosen for judgement and integrity as much as skill, trained before the studio opens rather than after. One manager covers eight studios at most — a ceiling, not a target.揀嘅係判斷力同誠信，唔止手藝；而且係開店之前培訓。一位管理者最多管八間工作室——呢個係上限，唔係目標。

The uncomfortable part唔舒服嘅部分 — hand-made studios cannot out-produce a factory, so atelier output alone caps this business at roughly US$5–6M. Volume belongs in a central workshop; the studios keep the work that cannot be standardised.手作工作室嘅效率永遠低過工廠，所以單靠名匠產能，呢門生意上限大約 US$5–6M。量產交給中央工坊，工作室只做無法標準化嘅部分。
