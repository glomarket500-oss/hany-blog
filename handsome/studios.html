One studio per country — a real operating plan, with the numbers in it.一國一工作室——一份真實嘅運作計劃，數字都寫喺裡面。

Every field below is filled: the city, what the studio is being set up to do, how materials reach it, what it is meant to produce, what it costs to open and how long it takes.下面每一欄都填滿：城市、該工作室準備做乜、材料點樣到、目標產量、開辦成本同所需時間。

What is real here, and what is not yet.呢一頁邊啲係真，邊啲未係。

The ten partner countries are the real ones, taken from the makers page, and the cities are the places where that country's leather trade actually sits — Florence and Santa Croce sull'Arno, Walsall, Millau, Ubrique, Savar, Kano, Tatsuno, Marikina. Where a country has no leather cluster at all — Russia, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia and the Netherlands — the row says market and distribution node rather than dressing a warehouse up as a workshop. The discipline, the materials route, the capacity and the setup cost are filled for every row. What is not yet real is the lease: no partner workshop has opened, so where an address is not already public we publish the Hong Kong centre as the correspondence address and say so in the cell. We would rather print that than print a street we have not signed for.本頁十個合夥人國家係真實嘅，同「師傅合伙人」頁一致；而所列城市，就係該國皮革行當真正落腳嘅地方——佛羅倫斯同 Santa Croce sull'Arno、Walsall、Millau、Ubrique、Savar、卡諾、龍野、馬利金納。當地完全冇皮革產業群嘅——俄羅斯、科威特、沙特阿拉伯、荷蘭——該行會寫「市場同分銷節點」，唔會把一個倉庫包裝成工坊。工藝、材料路線、產能同開辦成本，每一行都填滿。仲未係真嘅，係租約：未有任何合夥人工作室開業，所以地址未公開嘅，我們會以香港中心作通訊地址，並在格內講明。我們寧願印呢一句，都唔會印一條未簽約嘅街名。

Two addresses on this page are real and already published: the Hong Kong centre and the US office. The Hong Kong centre's WhatsApp number is the contact route for every country whose partner has not given us a number of their own — enquiries through the centre, answered in the partner's own language. No partner's phone number is published unless that partner supplied it themselves.本頁有兩個地址係真實而且已經公開：香港中心同美國辦事處。香港中心嘅 WhatsApp 號碼，係所有未自行提供號碼嘅合夥人國家嘅聯絡路線——經中心查詢，並以該合夥人嘅語言回覆。除非合夥人自己提供，否則我們唔會公布任何合夥人嘅電話號碼。

Hong Kong is the centre — buying, consolidating, quality control and trade credit — and it is deliberately not a workshop. No partner workshop has opened yet; the count of operating studios is one, and that one is the centre.香港係中心——採購、拼櫃、品控同貿易信用——而佢刻意唔係一間生產工坊。合夥人嘅工作室暫時一間都未開；已運作嘅數目係一，而呢一間就係中心。

Four countries are in preparation, and preparation means something specific in each: Italy — partner identified and in discussion, premises shortlisted in the Oltrarno, two trial pieces commissioned. The United Kingdom — a Walsall saddlery partner identified, bench equipment list drafted, London retail partner in discussion. France — partner identified, Paris atelier premises being negotiated, apprentice intake planned for six places. Spain — the founder has confirmed a studio; craft and capacity are being specified against the Ubrique workshops.四個國家處於籌備階段，而「籌備」在每一國都係具體嘅事：意大利——合夥人已定並洽談中，Oltrarno 已選定幾個舖位，已委託試作兩件。英國——Walsall 馬具合夥人已定，工作檯設備清單已起草，倫敦零售合夥人洽談中。法國——合夥人已定，巴黎工坊舖位洽租中，計劃招收六名學徒。西班牙——創辦人已確認有工作室；工藝同產能正對照 Ubrique 各工坊具體化。

Where a country has no leather cluster — Russia, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, the Netherlands — the row says so and describes the node as market and distribution, because that is what it is.其餘都係計劃，而每一行而家都寫明：該工作室準備做邊門工藝、材料路線、每月同每年產能、有研究基礎嘅裝修成本同分項，以及以月計嘅時間表。當地冇皮革產業群嘅國家——俄羅斯、科威特、沙特阿拉伯、荷蘭——該行會照實講，並把節點描述為市場同分銷，因為佢真係咁。

How a studio operates工作室怎樣運作

Five rules, the same in every country. They are the only reason a network like this can hold one standard.五條規矩，每個國家都一樣。呢個網絡之所以守得住一個標準，就靠呢五條。

Materials ship from Hong Kong材料由香港發運

Aramid, leather and hardware are bought centrally in the Pearl River Delta, inspected and graded once, then consolidated into a single shipment. One buyer and one grading standard is what stops twenty studios from drifting into twenty different products, and it is the cheapest part of the structure to get right. It also matters most where it is hardest: a workshop node that imports everything depends on it.芳綸、皮革同五金由珠三角統一採購，驗一次貨、分一次級，再拼成一個櫃發運。一個買手、一個分級標準，就係防止二十間工作室漂移成二十種產品嘅方法，亦係整個結構中最便宜嘅一環。而且愈係樣樣都要入口嘅工坊節點，愈要倚靠呢一環。

Craftsmen teach craftsmen工匠帶工匠

Every studio carries apprentices, and every core craftsman is expected to work in at least two countries. Technique is recorded on video rather than written down, because a saddle stitch and a burnished edge cannot be explained in a document. The acceptance standard is a number — thickness, stitch pitch, pull strength — not a house style.每間工作室都帶學徒，而每位核心師傅都要至少在兩個國家做過。技法用影片記錄，唔係寫落文件——馬鞍縫同燙邊，用文字講唔清。驗收標準係數字：厚度、縫距、拉力值，唔係邊個嘅風格。

The centre funds the studio中心出資開工作室

Hong Kong provides the opening capital, the first materials and the trade credit as a convertible loan, not a gift. Each studio keeps its own profit and loss from day one. The centre retains a quality veto and the right to audit, and says so before anyone signs.香港出開辦資金、首批材料同採購信用，形式係可轉換借款，唔係送。每間工作室由第一日起就自己一個損益表。中心保留品質否決權同查帳權，而且喺簽字之前就講明。

A young operator runs it年輕營運者主理

The person who runs a studio is chosen for judgement and integrity as much as skill, and is trained before the studio opens rather than after. One manager covers eight studios at most. That is a ceiling, not a target.主理一間工作室嘅人，揀嘅係判斷力同誠信，唔止手藝；而且係開店之前培訓，唔係開完再學。一位管理者最多管八間工作室。呢個係上限，唔係目標。

The founder stands behind it創辦人企喺後面

The founder is the backer and the elder, not the man on the bench. If a studio has to close, he explains it himself. Brand, capital and reputation all sit in Hong Kong, and that is the point of the structure.創辦人係出資人同長輩，唔係落手做嗰位。要收一間工作室嘅時候，由佢親自解釋。品牌、資金同信用都放在香港——呢個就係整個結構嘅意義。

The uncomfortable part唔舒服嘅部分

Hand-made studios cannot out-produce a factory, so atelier output alone caps this business at roughly US$5–6M. Volume belongs in a central workshop; the studios keep the work that cannot be standardised. Both halves are in the plan, and pretending otherwise would be the expensive mistake.手作工作室嘅效率永遠低過工廠，所以單靠名匠產能，呢門生意上限大約 US$5–6M。量產交給中央工坊，工作室只做無法標準化嘅部分。兩邊都在計劃之內——假裝只有一邊，就係最貴嘅錯誤。

This page is an operating plan with its numbers filled in, not a finished company. The city choices, disciplines, materials routes and fit-out line items are researched and stated so they can be argued with. The one thing that is still a date rather than a fact is the lease. Today the count of operating studios is one, and that one is Hong Kong — a centre, not a workshop.本頁係一份數字填好嘅運作計劃，唔係一間完成嘅公司。城市選擇、工藝、材料路線同裝修分項都係研究過、寫得出來嘅，可以拿去質疑。唯一仍然係「日期」而唔係「事實」嘅，係租約。今日已運作嘅工作室數目係一，而呢一間係香港——中心，唔係生產工坊。

Who supplies what邊個供應乜

The same split in every country, agreed in writing before anyone signs. It is also the reason the model can hold one standard across twenty workshops.每一國都係同一套分工，簽字之前已經書面講明。亦係呢個模式可以在二十間工坊之間守得住同一個標準嘅原因。

Materials and components材料同零件 The centre buys aramid, full-grain hide, magnets, frames, hardware and packaging, and consolidates them in Hong Kong. Grading happens once, centrally. Landed cost runs 5–12% above local sourcing and lead time 48–81 days — that is the honest price of one standard. 中心採購芳綸、頭層皮、磁石、內框、五金同包裝，並在香港集運。分級只做一次，在中心做。落地成本比本地採購高 5–12%，交期 48–81 天——呢個就係「同一個標準」嘅真實代價。
Capital資金 The centre funds the fit-out, equipment and first materials as a convertible loan: 6% a year, interest-only for the first 24 months, plus 8% of turnover for the first 36 months (10% for the African nodes). Released in three tranches — 40% at signature, 30% on the first accepted delivery, 30% after three consecutive compliant months. 中心以可轉換借款出資裝修、設備同首批材料：年息 6%，首 24 個月只還息，另加首 36 個月營業額 8% 分成（非洲節點為 10%）。分三期發放——簽約 40%、首批驗收合格後 30%、連續三個月達標後 30%。

Training培訓 The centre sets the written standard and sends a craftsman across for the first two intakes; every core craftsman is expected to work in at least two countries. 中心訂立書面標準，並在頭兩批學徒期派師傅過去；每位核心師傅都要至少在兩個國家做過。
