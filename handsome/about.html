We only do three things.我們只做三件事。

HANDSOME exists because the phone case market has two ends and nothing useful in between: $5 cases that fall apart, and $70 cases that spend most of the money on marketing.HANDSOME 的存在，是因為手機殼市場只有兩端，中間是空的：5 美元用幾週就壞的殼，和 70 美元但錢大多花在市場推廣上的殼。

What we stand for我們的立場

Say the material材質要講明

If a case is polycarbonate, we call it polycarbonate. “Aerospace-grade” and “military-grade” mean nothing without a number and a lab behind them.是 PC 就寫 PC。「航空級」「軍工級」如果沒有數字和實驗室支撐，等於沒說。

Publish the limits限制也要寫

Every product page lists who it is not for. A case that claims to do everything usually does nothing well.每個產品頁都寫明它不適合誰。什麼都能做的殼，通常什麼都做不好。

Price the material按材質定價

$15–$50 across the range, and the gap between the tiers is the material cost, not the size of the logo.全線 15–50 美元，價差來自材質成本，不是 logo 的大小。

Sell to you, not past you賣給你，不是繞過你

We would rather answer an email and lose a sale than take an order we cannot fulfil.我們寧願回一封 email 少做一單，也不接做不了的訂單。

Where we make我們在哪裡做

Phone case manufacturing is concentrated in the Pearl River Delta, and that is where our production partners are. Working close to the factories is why three materials are possible at this price — not a shortcut, a location.手機殼製造集中在珠三角，我們的生產夥伴就在那裡。能在那個價位做到三種材質，靠的是離工廠近，不是走捷徑。
