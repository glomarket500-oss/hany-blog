Two capacities, verified differently兩種身份，兩種驗證方法

Most of our partners are both. They are not the same job, and we do not check them the same way.我們大部分合夥人兩種身份都有。但兩者唔係同一件事，我們亦唔會用同一種方法去核。

The craftsman is judged by the hand師傅，用雙手嚟秤

A craftsman makes things. That is a claim about skill, so it is verified by skill: ten to twenty finished pieces seen in person, including the failures; then one paid trial piece against a written brief with stated tolerances, paid at a fair rate whether or not we go on; then two references we can actually reach. Nobody passes on the strength of a portfolio photograph.師傅係做嘢嘅人。呢個係關於手藝嘅講法，所以只可以用手藝去驗：十至二十件成品當面睇，連失敗品一齊睇；然後按一份寫明公差嘅書面要求做一件有償試作，無論最後合唔合作都照付合理報酬；再查兩位真係搵得到嘅推薦人。淨靠 portfolio 相，唔會過關。

The country agent is judged by trust各國代理，用信用嚟秤

An agent carries our money, our materials and our name into a country we are not standing in. That is a claim about reliability, so it is verified by a history: small staged orders first, then larger ones; delivered on the day promised; mistakes corrected without being chased; and the customs and payment paperwork clean every time. No single transaction proves it — a run of them does.代理嘅人，會將我們嘅錢、我們嘅材料同我們嘅名，帶去一個我們唔在場嘅國家。呢個係關於可靠嘅講法，所以只可以用紀錄去驗：先細單、後大單，分階段落；講幾時到就幾時到；出錯唔使人追都肯改；報關同收款文件每次都乾淨。一單生意證明唔到，一連串先證明得到。

Why we keep the two apart on the page: a good agent is not automatically a craftsman, and a good craftsman is not automatically someone you hand a country to. Until the founder confirms each partner's capacity, both tags stay TBC and no card calls anyone a craftsman.點解要喺版面上分開講：好代理唔自動等於好師傅，好師傅亦唔自動等於可以交一個國家畀佢。在創辦人逐一確認之前，兩個標籤都會維持「待定」，冇一張卡會叫任何人做師傅。

How we choose a partner我們怎樣選一位合夥人

Four gates, in this order. Nobody skips one because their portfolio is beautiful.四道關，次序唔可以調。portfolio 靚唔會令人跳關。

1. Portfolio review一、作品審查

Ten to twenty finished pieces, seen in person, plus the failures. We look for consistency across a run, not one showcase piece.十至二十件成品，當面睇，連失敗品一齊睇。我們睇嘅係一批貨之間嘅一致性，唔係一件招牌作品。

2. A paid trial piece二、有償試作

One piece against a written brief with stated tolerances, paid at a fair rate whether or not we continue. The brief is the test, not the sales pitch.按一份寫明公差嘅書面要求做一件，無論最後合唔合作都照付合理報酬。測驗嘅係佢點跟要求，唔係佢點推銷自己。

3. References三、查推薦人

Two named references we can actually reach — a previous client and a previous employer or master. Delivered on time, corrected mistakes, stayed for the boring part.兩位真係搵得到嘅推薦人——一位舊客戶、一位舊僱主或師傅。問嘅係：交得準時嗎？出錯肯改嗎？悶嘅工序肯留低做嗎？

4. Staged trust四、分階段信任

Small orders first, then larger ones, then a studio. Eighteen to twenty-four months is normal here. It is also the cheapest part of the process.先細單，後大單，最後才給工作室。十八到二十四個月係正常速度，而呢段時間係整個流程中最便宜嘅成本。

Where the process stands today: the partner network is real and its photographs are on this page, but the four gates above have not yet been run on every partner, and no partner's craft capacity has been confirmed. We are not going to describe a partner as a craftsman because his photograph sits next to a craft description. When each partner's discipline, years, lineage and studio status are confirmed, they replace the TBC fields on that card.今日嘅進度：合夥人網絡係真實嘅，相亦已經放上本頁；但上面四道關未在每位合夥人身上跑完，而亦未有邊位合夥人嘅手藝身份獲得確認。我們唔會因為一張相放喺工序描述旁邊，就形容嗰位合夥人係師傅。每位合夥人嘅專長、年資、師承同工作室狀態一經確認，就會取代佢張卡上面嘅「待定」欄。
