Twelve partner cards, ten countries, one written standard.十二張合夥人卡、十個國家、一套寫出嚟嘅標準。

Read this before you read the cards.睇下面嘅卡之前，請先睇呢段。

This page is a working document, not a finished shelf.呢一頁係一份工作中嘅文件，唔係一個擺好晒嘅貨架。

Every photograph below is of an actual partner of this company, in ten countries, and nothing on this page is filled in with a placeholder. The craft standard is written out in full further down — twelve disciplines, each with the acceptance criteria we measure against, drawn from how Hermès' leather workshop, the Compagnons du Devoir, the Florentine scuole and the UK leather-craft apprenticeship define the same work. Each country's card states what that country actually has: a tannery base, an export leather-goods industry, a saddlery bench, or simply a market we sell into. Where a country has no craft tradition, the card says so and the partner is listed as a country agent, because that is the honest description.下面每一張相，都係本公司真實合夥人嘅照片，來自十個國家；本頁冇任何一欄係用佔位字填嘅。手藝標準全文寫喺下面——十二門學科，每一門都列出我們量度嘅驗收標準，參照愛馬仕皮具工坊、Compagnons du Devoir、佛羅倫斯嘅皮革學校同英國皮革工藝學徒制點樣定義同一批工夫。每個國家嘅卡都寫明當地真正有乜：製革基礎、出口皮具工業、馬具工作檯，抑或只係一個我們賣貨嘅市場。當地冇手工藝傳統嘅，卡上照實講，該位合夥人就列為「各國代理」——因為咁先係誠實嘅描述。

Two kinds of statement live on these cards and we do not blur them. Statements about the trade or the country — what the disciplines are, what each country's industry is, what a lineage consists of — are researched and sourced. Statements about what a role requires — years at the bench, monthly output — are stated as the benchmark for that role, with the basis given; the founder knows these partners personally and will correct any figure he knows better. No partner's name and no partner's phone number appears anywhere on this site.呢啲卡上嘅講法分兩種，我們唔會混為一談。關於行業或國家嘅講法——學科係乜、每個國家嘅產業係乜、師承包含啲乜——係研究過、有出處嘅。關於角色要求嘅講法——年資、月產量——就寫成該角色嘅基準值，並註明依據；創辦人親自認識呢批合夥人，任何他知得更清楚嘅數字，他會改。本頁冇任何合夥人姓名，亦冇任何合夥人電話號碼。

The craft standard: twelve disciplines, and what passing looks like手藝標準：十二門學科，以及「合格」係點

This is the bench every partner is measured on. The wording of each discipline comes from how the established houses and trade schools define the same work; the acceptance criteria are the numbers we actually check.呢個就係量度每一位合夥人嘅工作檯。每一門學科嘅講法，參照老牌工坊同行業學校點樣定義同一批工夫；驗收標準就係我們真正會查嘅數字。

Pattern making and sampling版型與打樣

Reading a phone model into a flat pattern, then a three-dimensional one: working drawings, a paper pattern, a card mock-up, and a sample that is measured before anything is cut. Tolerance on a phone case is tight — the case has to clear the camera bump, the buttons and the charging port, and still grip.由手機型號讀出平面版型，再讀成立體：工作圖、紙樣、卡紙試模，然後一件量度過才落刀嘅樣品。手機殼公差很窄——要避開鏡頭凸台、按鍵同充電口，同時要夾得住。

What good looks like:合格係點： Pattern within ±0.5 mm of the working drawing; a mock-up that assembles dry before any leather is cut; every new model proven on a real handset, not a drawing.版型與工作圖相差 ±0.5 毫米以內；落皮之前先用卡紙乾裝一次；每個新型號都要用真機試過，唔可以靠圖。

Clicking and cutting裁切（開料）

Laying the pattern on the hide so the grain runs the right way, the stretch goes where the case flexes, and the cleanest part of the hide lands on the most visible panel. Cut by hand knife, by die and press, or by a travelling head cutter.把版型排在皮上：粒面方向要對、伸縮要落在殼會屈曲嘅位置、最靚嘅一塊皮要留給最顯眼嘅面板。可以用手刀、模具壓切，或者走刀機。

What good looks like:合格係點： Cut edges square to the surface with no wedge; no cut closer than 15 mm to the hide edge; the usable area of a hide actually measured, not assumed.切口與皮面垂直、唔起斜；離皮邊最少 15 毫米唔落刀；一張皮實際可用面積要量出來，唔可以估。

Skiving and splitting削薄與分層

Thinning leather where it will be folded, lapped or stitched over, so the seam does not become a ridge. A skiving knife or a bell-knife splitting machine takes the flesh side down; the grain side is left alone.喺會摺、會疊、會縫過嘅位置把皮削薄，令縫位唔會腫起一條脊。用削皮刀或者鐘形分層機削肉面，粒面唔郁。

What good looks like:合格係點： Fold edges down to roughly 0.4–0.6 mm and even along the length; no nicks through the grain; the transition sanded so it cannot be felt through the lining.摺邊削到約 0.4–0.6 毫米，而且全程均勻；唔可以削穿粒面；過渡位要磨順，隔住內襯都摸唔到。

Leather selection and tannage選皮與鞣製知識

Knowing what a hide is before it is cut: which tannage (vegetable, chrome, wet-white), which part of the hide (back, belly, shoulder), what the grain will do over a year, and how the batch will age together. Vegetable-tanned full-grain is the firm, patina-forming material the Tuscany district is built on.落刀之前要知張皮係乜：邊種鞣法（植鞣、鉻鞣、白濕皮）、邊個部位（背、腹、肩）、粒面一年之後會變成點，以及同一批皮會唔會一齊變色。植鞣頭層皮硬淨、會養成，就係托斯卡納皮革區嘅根基。

What good looks like:合格係點： Hides graded and recorded by batch before use; defects marked, not cut around silently; a leather case's panels cut from the same hide so the patina matches.皮要分級、要記錄批次才用；有瑕疵要標出來，唔可以靜靜地避開；同一個殼嘅面板要出自同一張皮，包漿才會一致。

Lining and reinforcement內襯與補強

Building the inside of the case: a lining that will not stretch, a reinforcement where the case takes its load, and an interlining that keeps the shell from going floppy in a pocket.做殼嘅裡面：一層唔會拉長嘅內襯、落喺受力位嘅補強，同一層令殼唔會放袋就軟嘅夾層。

What good looks like:合格係點： Lining cut 1:1 with the outer panel and stitched in, not glued floating; reinforcement positioned where the case flexes, not where it is convenient.內襯要同外板 1:1 裁、要縫實，唔可以浮起黏住；補強要落喺殼真正屈曲嘅位置，唔係落喺方便嘅位置。

Adhesion and lamination黏合與貼合

Joining leather to leather, or leather to a structural frame, without the glue line showing through the grain and without the bond failing at the edge. Contact cement, water-based adhesive or heat-activated film, applied evenly and pressed, not squeezed.把皮貼皮，或者把皮貼上結構內框：膠線唔可以透出粒面，邊位亦唔可以分層。用接觸膠、水性膠或熱熔膜，塗勻、加壓，唔可以夾硬擠。

What good looks like:合格係點： A glue line that does not telegraph through the grain under raking light; over-spray cleaned before it cures; edges still clean after a year of temperature cycling.斜光之下，膠線唔可以透出粒面；溢出嘅膠要在乾之前清走；經過一年溫度變化，邊位仍然乾淨。

Saddle stitching馬鞍縫（雙針手縫）

Two needles, one waxed thread, each stitch locked by hand through holes made with a pricking iron or an awl. This is the stitch Hermès' leather workshop is described by, and it is the reason a hand-made seam cannot unravel the way a machine lockstitch can.兩支針、一條上蠟線，每個針步都靠人手在菱斬或錐打嘅孔上鎖死。愛馬仕皮具工坊之所以為人所知，就係呢一手縫法；亦係手縫唔會像機器鎖縫一樣整條散開嘅原因。

What good looks like:合格係點： 8–10 stitches per inch for a bag-weight seam, thread matched to the leather weight, uniform pitch and tension front and back, back-stitched two stitches at every run end.袋裝厚度嘅縫約每英寸 8–10 針；線要配皮厚；正反兩面針距同張力要一致；每一段收針要回針兩針。

Edge finishing邊位修飾

The whole sequence, in order: trim flush, bevel the arris, sand through the grits, slick or burnish with gum and friction, then seal or edge-paint if the design calls for it. It is the slowest part of the piece and the first thing a customer's thumb finds.完整一輪，次序唔可以亂：修齊、削角、逐級砂紙磨、上膠水或蠟用摩擦燙邊，需要就上邊油封邊。呢一步係整件作品最慢嘅部分，亦係客人手指第一時間摸到嘅位。

What good looks like:合格係點： Edge rounded to roughly 1.5–2.0 mm radius and uniform along the whole run; colour sealed so it does not bleed onto the grain; no sanding scratches left at the transition.邊位圓角約 1.5–2.0 毫米、全程一致；封色唔可以滲上粒面；過渡位唔可以留低砂紙痕。

Aramid lay-up and moulding芳綸層疊與成型

The fibre side of the range: cutting aramid fabric on the bias or warp, stacking plies in a stated direction, wetting out with resin or laying pre-preg, then curing under heat and pressure in a matched mould so the shell takes the handset's shape and the magnet array sits true.纖維線嘅部分：按斜紋或經向裁芳綸布、按指定方向疊層、用樹脂浸透或疊預浸料，再在對模中加熱加壓固化，令殼體食住手機形狀，磁石陣列亦要擺正。

What good looks like:合格係點： Ply count and fibre direction recorded per part; resin fully wet out with no dry patches or voids; cured thickness held to the drawing; magnet ring checked for alignment and polarity before assembly.每一件都要記錄層數同纖維方向；樹脂要浸透，唔可以有乾斑或空洞；固化後厚度要對圖；磁環組裝前要驗位置同極性。

Hardware and assembly五金與組裝

Setting the last parts: magnets, plates, buttons, the frame, the camera bezel. Position, depth and alignment all decide whether the case works or merely looks like it works.裝最後嘅零件：磁石、金屬片、按鍵、內框、鏡頭圈。位置、深度同對齊，決定個殼係真係用得，抑或只係睇落似得。

What good looks like:合格係點： Magnets flush and aligned to the coil so charging is not throttled; every button working through the leather without shimming; no hardware proud enough to scratch a table.磁石要平、要對正線圈，唔可以令充電降速；每個按鍵隔住皮都要按得順；五金唔可以凸出到刮花桌面。

Quality inspection品質檢驗

The gate between the bench and the box: dimensional check against the drawing, stitch pitch and pull test, edge and lining inspection, magnet function, and a written record of who made it and from which batch.工作檯同包裝盒之間嗰道閘：對圖量尺寸、驗針距同拉力、檢查邊位同內襯、試磁吸功能，並記錄低係邊個做、用咗邊一批料。

What good looks like:合格係點： First-pass yield at or above 95%, rising to 97% before a partner is scaled up; sample inspection to a stated AQL; no piece shipped unrecorded.一次通過率要 95% 或以上，擴大規模前要升至 97%；抽檢要按寫明嘅 AQL；冇一件貨可以無記錄出廠。

Tooling and maintenance工具與保養

Keeping the bench able to hold the standard: sharpening knives and irons, dressing edge tools, setting and servicing the sewing, splitting and pressing machines, and controlling the workshop's own humidity so leather does not mould and glue does not fail.令張工作檯守得住標準：磨刀同菱斬、修整邊位工具、校準同保養縫紉機、分層機同壓合機，並控制工坊本身嘅濕度，令皮唔發霉、膠唔失效。

What good looks like:合格係點： A written maintenance log per machine; tool sharpening on a schedule rather than when it tears; workshop held at a stable humidity, with leather stored off the floor.每部機有保養紀錄；工具按時間表磨，唔係等到撕皮才磨；工坊濕度要穩，皮要離地存放。

Where the standard comes from: Hermès' leather workshop is documented as selecting hides, cutting, skiving to paper-thin edges, gluing a lining between them, marking with a compass, saddle-stitching with two needles and flaxen thread, then softening and sanding the edge. The Compagnons du Devoir run the maroquinerie and saddlery trades through the Tour de France, with a chef-d'œuvre as the final proof. The Scuola del Cuoio in Florence teaches the same sequence from hand tools upward. The UK's Leather Craftsperson apprenticeship standard and France's CAP Maroquinerie both list cutting, skiving, closing, edge finishing, material knowledge and inspection as the assessable units. We did not invent this list; we copied it and then wrote down what we measure.標準嘅出處：愛馬仕皮具工坊嘅流程有文獻記載——選皮、裁切、削到紙咁薄嘅邊、中間黏一層內襯、用圓規劃線、兩支針配麻線上馬鞍縫，最後磨軟同砂磨邊位。Compagnons du Devoir 以 Tour de France 巡迴方式傳承皮具同馬具行當，最後以一件 chef-d'œuvre 代表作定生死。佛羅倫斯 Scuola del Cuoio 由手工具開始教同一套次序。英國 Leather Craftsperson 學徒標準同法國 CAP Maroquinerie，都把裁切、削薄、縫合、邊位修飾、材料知識同檢驗列為考核單元。呢張清單唔係我們發明嘅——我們照抄，然後寫低我們量啲乜。

Two capacities, verified differently兩種身份，兩種驗證方法

Most of our partners are both. They are not the same job, and we do not check them the same way.我們大部分合夥人兩種身份都有。但兩者唔係同一件事，我們亦唔會用同一種方法去核。

The craftsman is judged by the hand師傅，用雙手嚟秤

A craftsman makes things. That is a claim about skill, so it is verified by skill: ten to twenty finished pieces seen in person, including the failures; then one paid trial piece against a written brief with stated tolerances, paid at a fair rate whether or not we go on; then two references we can actually reach. Nobody passes on the strength of a portfolio photograph.師傅係做嘢嘅人。呢個係關於手藝嘅講法，所以只可以用手藝去驗：十至二十件成品當面睇，連失敗品一齊睇；然後按一份寫明公差嘅書面要求做一件有償試作，無論最後合唔合作都照付合理報酬；再查兩位真係搵得到嘅推薦人。淨靠 portfolio 相，唔會過關。

The country agent is judged by trust各國代理，用信用嚟秤

An agent carries our money, our materials and our name into a country we are not standing in. That is a claim about reliability, so it is verified by a history: small staged orders first, then larger ones; delivered on the day promised; mistakes corrected without being chased; and the customs and payment paperwork clean every time. No single transaction proves it — a run of them does.代理嘅人，會將我們嘅錢、我們嘅材料同我們嘅名，帶去一個我們唔在場嘅國家。呢個係關於可靠嘅講法，所以只可以用紀錄去驗：先細單、後大單，分階段落；講幾時到就幾時到；出錯唔使人追都肯改；報關同收款文件每次都乾淨。一單生意證明唔到，一連串先證明得到。

How to read the two tags on a card. A country with a tannery base, an export leather-goods industry or a saddlery bench — Italy, France, the United Kingdom, Bangladesh, Nigeria, Uganda — carries the craftsman tag, because that is work the country can actually do. A country we sell into and distribute from, with no bespoke leather tradition of its own — Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, the Netherlands, and the Russian cards — carries the country-agent tag only. The tag describes the role, not a compliment: calling a distributor a craftsman would be the easiest lie on this page, and it is the one we have refused to tell.卡上兩個標籤點睇。有製革基礎、出口皮具工業或馬具工作檯嘅國家——意大利、法國、英國、孟加拉、尼日利亞、烏干達——帶「師傅」標籤，因為嗰啲工夫係當地真正做得到嘅。我們賣貨同分銷、但本身冇訂造皮具傳統嘅國家——沙特阿拉伯、科威特、荷蘭，以及俄羅斯兩張卡——只帶「各國代理」標籤。個標籤講嘅係角色，唔係一句好話：把一個分銷商叫做師傅，會係本頁最容易講嘅一句大話，而呢一句我們拒絕講。

How we choose a partner我們怎樣選一位合夥人

Four gates, in this order. Nobody skips one because their portfolio is beautiful.四道關，次序唔可以調。portfolio 靚唔會令人跳關。

1. Portfolio review一、作品審查

Ten to twenty finished pieces, seen in person, plus the failures. We look for consistency across a run, not one showcase piece.十至二十件成品，當面睇，連失敗品一齊睇。我們睇嘅係一批貨之間嘅一致性，唔係一件招牌作品。

2. A paid trial piece二、有償試作

One piece against a written brief with stated tolerances, paid at a fair rate whether or not we continue. The brief is the test, not the sales pitch.按一份寫明公差嘅書面要求做一件，無論最後合唔合作都照付合理報酬。測驗嘅係佢點跟要求，唔係佢點推銷自己。

3. References三、查推薦人

Two named references we can actually reach — a previous client and a previous employer or master. Delivered on time, corrected mistakes, stayed for the boring part.兩位真係搵得到嘅推薦人——一位舊客戶、一位舊僱主或師傅。問嘅係：交得準時嗎？出錯肯改嗎？悶嘅工序肯留低做嗎？

4. Staged trust四、分階段信任

Small orders first, then larger ones, then a studio. Eighteen to twenty-four months is normal here. It is also the cheapest part of the process.先細單，後大單，最後才給工作室。十八到二十四個月係正常速度，而呢段時間係整個流程中最便宜嘅成本。

Where the process stands today: the standard above is written and published, the partner network is real and its photographs are on this page, and every card now states a discipline, a studio location and a target capacity instead of a blank.
