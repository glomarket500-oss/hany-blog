We are building the workshop network one studio at a time, and the first studio has not opened. So the six profiles below are placeholders. The names are placeholders (“Maker A — TBC”), the years and lineages are empty fields, and the portraits are AI-generated stand-ins — hands and workbenches, deliberately never an identifiable face, because we are not going to invent a craftsman and then give him a face. Leather cutting & hand-stitching皮革裁切與手縫

Cutting decides what a hide becomes: which panel takes the stress, which one takes the camera aperture, and which is waste. Stitching a case by hand means a saddle stitch — two needles and one waxed thread, locked at every hole. If a stitch breaks, the seam holds.裁皮決定一張皮變成乜：邊幅受力、邊幅留鏡頭位、邊幅要棄。手縫即係馬鞍縫——兩支針、一條上蠟線，每個孔都互相鎖死。斷一針，條縫都唔會散。

Leatherworking & edge finishing皮具成型與邊緣處理

Edge finishing is the least forgiving part of leatherwork. The edge is cut, bevelled, sanded through several grits, then burnished with wax until it is sealed and round. There is nowhere to hide: a bad edge is visible from across a room, and it is the first thing to fail once the leather gets wet.邊緣處理係皮具最唔留情面嘅一道工序：切、削、由粗到細磨幾道砂紙，再上蠟燙到封口變圓。冇得遮——一條差嘅邊，隔一個房間都睇得出，而一沾水，最先爛嘅就係佢。

Pattern making & splitting紙樣與削皮

Ubrique has cut and stitched leather for other people's labels for generations, so the discipline here is tolerance. A paper pattern has to survive a few hundred cuttings without drifting, and split leather has to come out within a tenth of a millimetre or the case will not close cleanly.烏布里克幾代人都在替人家嘅品牌裁皮、縫皮，所以呢度嘅本事係公差：一張紙樣要捱得住幾百次裁剪都唔走位，削皮要削到十分一毫米之內，唔係個殼就合唔埋。

Aramid lay-up & pattern joining芳綸層疊與版型拼接

Aramid arrives as woven sheet and prepreg, and someone has to decide the ply count, the fibre direction of each layer, and where the weave is allowed to run out at the corners. Lay-up is where thinness is won or lost: one extra ply is a case that resists the pocket.芳綸嚟到嘅時候係織布同預浸料，要有人決定疊幾層、每層纖維行乜方向、邊個位可以走紗走到角落。薄唔薄，就係喺層疊嗰一刻決定：多一層，個殼就開始同你嘅口袋作對。

Assembly & quality inspection組裝與品檢

Assembly joins the material to the frame, the magnet array and the camera lip. Inspection decides whether a piece leaves the building. How we choose a craftsman我們怎樣選一位師傅

Four gates, in this order. Nobody skips one because their portfolio is beautiful.四道關，次序唔可以調。portfolio 靚唔會令人跳關。

1. Portfolio review一、作品審查

Ten to twenty finished pieces, seen in person, plus the failures. We look for consistency across a run, not one showcase piece.十至二十件成品，當面睇，連失敗品一齊睇。我們睇嘅係一批貨之間嘅一致性，唔係一件招牌作品。

2. A paid trial piece二、有償試作

One piece against a written brief with stated tolerances, paid at a fair rate whether or not we continue. The brief is the test, not the sales pitch.按一份寫明公差嘅書面要求做一件，無論最後合唔合作都照付合理報酬。測驗嘅係佢點跟要求，唔係佢點推銷自己。

3. References三、查推薦人

Two named references we can actually reach — a previous client and a previous employer or master. Delivered on time, corrected mistakes, stayed for the boring part.兩位真係搵得到嘅推薦人——一位舊客戶、一位舊僱主或師傅。問嘅係：交得準時嗎？出錯肯改嗎？悶嘅工序肯留低做嗎？

4. Staged trust四、分階段信任

Small orders first, then larger ones, then a studio. Eighteen to twenty-four months is normal here. It is also the cheapest part of the process.先細單，後大單，最後才給工作室。十八到二十四個月係正常速度，而呢段時間係整個流程中最便宜嘅成本。
