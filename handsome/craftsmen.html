How the network is run網絡點樣運作

Three short answers: what the two roles are, how we verify them, and how a partner is chosen.三條短答：兩種身份係乜、我們點核、以及我們點揀一位合夥人。

Two capacities, verified differently兩種身份，兩種驗證方法

Most of our partners are both craftsman and country agent, and we do not check them the same way. The craftsman is judged by the hand: ten to twenty finished pieces seen in person including the failures, then one paid trial piece against a written brief with stated tolerances, then two references we can actually reach. The agent is judged by a history: small staged orders first, delivered on the day promised, mistakes corrected without being chased, and the customs and payment paperwork clean every time.我們大部分合夥人兩種身份都有，而我們唔會用同一種方法去核。師傅用手藝去驗：十至二十件成品當面睇（連失敗品），然後按寫明公差嘅書面要求做一件有償試作，再查兩位真係搵得到嘅推薦人。代理用紀錄去驗：先細單、後大單，講幾時到就幾時到，出錯唔使人追都肯改，報關同收款文件每次都乾淨。

How to read the two tags in a card: a country with a tannery base, an export leather-goods industry or a saddlery bench carries the craftsman tag, because that is work the country can actually do. A country we sell into and distribute from, with no bespoke leather tradition of its own, carries the country-agent tag only. Calling a distributor a craftsman would be the easiest lie on this page, and it is the one we refused to tell.卡上兩個標籤點睇：有製革基礎、出口皮具工業或馬具工作檯嘅國家帶「師傅」標籤，因為嗰啲工夫係當地真正做得到嘅。我們賣貨同分銷、但本身冇訂造皮具傳統嘅國家，只帶「各國代理」標籤。把一個分銷商叫做師傅，會係本頁最容易講嘅一句大話，而呢一句我們拒絕講。

How we choose a partner: four gates我們怎樣選一位合夥人：四道關

1. Portfolio review一、作品審查 — ten to twenty finished pieces, seen in person, plus the failures. We look for consistency across a run, not one showcase piece.十至二十件成品，當面睇，連失敗品一齊睇。我們睇嘅係一批貨之間嘅一致性，唔係一件招牌作品。

2. A paid trial piece二、有償試作 — one piece against a written brief with stated tolerances, paid at a fair rate whether or not we continue. The brief is the test, not the sales pitch.按一份寫明公差嘅書面要求做一件，無論最後合唔合作都照付合理報酬。測驗嘅係佢點跟要求，唔係佢點推銷自己。

3. References三、查推薦人 — two named references we can actually reach — a previous client and a previous employer or master.兩位真係搵得到嘅推薦人——一位舊客戶、一位舊僱主或師傅。

4. Staged trust四、分階段信任 — small orders first, then larger ones. Eighteen to twenty-four months is normal here, and it is also the cheapest part of the process.先細單，後大單。十八到二十四個月係正常速度，亦係整個流程中最便宜嘅成本。

Where this stands today: the standard is published on the craft page, the partner network is real, and every card states a discipline and a target capacity instead of a blank. What is still in progress is the audit trail behind the numbers — each partner's own documented years and the individual who trained them. We publish the target and its basis, and update the card when the document exists.今日嘅進度：標準已喺「手藝標準」頁公開，合夥人網絡係真實嘅，而每張卡都寫明專長同目標產能，唔再留白。仍然進行中嘅，係數字背後嘅憑證——每位合夥人自己嘅可證明年資，同帶佢出身嘅那個人。我們公開目標同目標嘅依據，文件一到就更新張卡。
