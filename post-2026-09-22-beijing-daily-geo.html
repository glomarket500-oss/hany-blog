《當主流媒體認真回答「AI 時代我憑什麼存在」——北京日報 GEO 服務嘅啟示》

鄭哥 Hany · 2026 年 9 月 22 日 · 福田 · 沙丘咖啡

引子

今朝坐喺沙丘咖啡，飲緊一杯 espresso，朋友嚟之前，我睇到一單新聞——

北京日報推 GEO 服務、900 家信源聯盟 + 智譜合作

睇完之後，我哋傾咗成個鐘。呢個新聞其實挺有看頭嘅，我俾你掰扯掰扯。

第一點：媒體轉型裏少見嘅「真動作」

呢些年「媒體融合」「系統性變革」喊咗唔少，但大多停喺將報紙搬上 APP。

北京日報今次唔一樣——佢瞄準嘅係 GEO（生成式引擎優化） 呢個新賽道。

講白咗：

而家越嚟越多人有問題唔搜百度，直接問 AI。

咁媒體嘅權威內容可唔可以出現喺 AI 嘅答案入面？呢個就係 GEO 要做嘅事。

鄭哥嘅判斷係準嘅——

流量入口正在從「搜索框」遷移到「對話框」

媒體如果仲守 SEO 嗰套老地圖，等於喺新戰場上攞舊地圖搵路。

第二點：商業邏輯其實精明

你細睇佢嘅服務清單：

  • ✅ 官網診斷
  • ✅ 現狀監測
  • ✅ 內容創作
  • ✅ 信源發佈
  • ✅ 效果預期

呢個係一整套可以賣錢嘅 GEO 諮詢服務。

翻譯一下：

媒體將幾十年攞嘅公信力，包裝成咗 AI 時代嘅產品。

以前公信力係「無形資產」，而家直接變成咗收費能力。

呢個由「賣版面賣流量」到「賣可信度」嘅轉身，係呢篇新聞入面最值錢嘅信息。

第三點：900 家信源聯盟係關鍵一子

單家媒體嘅內容量餵唔飽大模型，但：

  • ✅ 100 多個政務號 + 600 多個北京號 = 900+ 信源聯盟
  • ✅ 變成有分量嘅語料池

呢個係喺解決AI 行業最頭疼嘅問題：

  • ⚠️ 幻覺（hallucination）
  • ⚠️ 信源污染
一邊係模型缺乾淨語料，一邊係媒體缺新場景，兩邊一拍即合。

同智譜嘅合作都係呢個邏輯：

  • ✅ 智譜出權威內容 + 治理經驗
  • ✅ 智譜出模型 + 算法
  • ✅ 拼圖啱啱拼上

兩句潑冷水嘅話

冷水 1：「可信傳播」可唔可以真落地？

  • ⚠️ 發佈會上講得好靚
  • ⚠️ AI 答案層嘅影響力點樣量化？
  • ⚠️ 客戶買單後效果點樣兌現？
  • ⚠️ 呢套「效果預期」嘅驗證體系仲未經過市場檢驗

要再觀察。

冷水 2：跟風者會湧進來

  • ⚠️ 賽道一熱，各地媒體估計很快出類似方案
  • ⚠️ 到時拚嘅係真本事：語料質量、技術底座、聯盟規模

而唔係發佈會規格。

一句話總結

主流媒體第一次認真地回答了「AI 時代我憑什麼存在」呢個問題——答案唔係對抗 AI，而係成為 AI 答案入面嗰個「可信嘅信源」。

方向對了，路還長。

對鄭哥 4 大業務嘅啟示

1. 電子消費品（音箱 / AI 終端）

  • ✅ AI 答案層 = 新流量入口
  • ✅ 內容權威性 = AI 時代嘅新護城河
  • ✅ 跨境電商要關注 GEO 優化

2. AI 算力

  • ✅ 乾淨語料 = AI 公司剛需
  • ✅ 權威信源 = 稀缺資源
  • ✅ 算力客戶由「模型廠」擴展到「媒體」

3. 跨境電商

  • ✅ 海外媒體聯盟 = 跨境 GEO
  • ✅ 小語種權威信源 = 跨境 AI 答案

4. 年輕人投資

  • ✅ 智譜 / 大模型 + 媒體 = 投資標的
  • ✅ 信源質量 = AI 公司嘅護城河
  • ✅ GEO 服務賽道 = 新嘅創業機會

"What Makes Mainstream Media Worth Keeping Around in the AI Era — Lessons from Beijing Daily's GEO Service"

Hany Zheng Ge · September 22, 2026 · Shaqu Cafe, Futian, Shenzhen

Setup

This morning at Shaqu Cafe in Futian, sipping an espresso before a friend arrived, I came across a news item —

Beijing Daily launches GEO service, 900-source alliance, partnership with Zhipu AI

We ended up talking about it for an hour. There's actually a lot to unpack here.

Point 1: A rare "real move" in media转型

Over the past few years, slogans like "media convergence" and "systemic transformation" have been everywhere — but most of these efforts just amounted to putting the newspaper onto an app.

Beijing Daily's move is different — they're targeting the new lane called GEO (Generative Engine Optimization).

Plain English:

More and more people, when they have a question, skip Baidu and go straight to an AI.

So the question becomes: can authoritative media content show up in the AI's answer? That's what GEO is about.

Hany's read is right on this:

Traffic is migrating from "search box" to "chat box."

If media outlets keep relying on the SEO playbook, they're bringing an old map to a new battlefield.

Point 2: The business logic is actually sharp

Look closely at their service list:

  • ✅ Official-site audit
  • ✅ Status monitoring
  • ✅ Content production
  • ✅ Source publishing
  • ✅ Outcome forecasting

This is a whole package of GEO consulting you can charge for.

Translation:

Media is packaging decades of accumulated credibility into a product for the AI era.

Credibility used to be an "intangible asset." Now it has become a direct billing capability.

This pivot from "selling columns and traffic" to "selling credibility" is the most valuable insight in this news item.

Point 3: The 900-source alliance is the key move

A single media outlet can't feed a large model enough content — but:

  • ✅ 100+ government accounts + 600+ Beijing accounts = 900+ source alliance
  • ✅ Becomes a substantive corpus

This solves AI's most painful problem:

  • ⚠️ Hallucination
  • ⚠️ Source contamination
Models need clean corpora; media need new contexts. Both sides click.

The Zhipu partnership follows the same logic:

  • ✅ Zhipu provides authoritative content + governance experience
  • ✅ Zhipu provides model + algorithm
  • ✅ The puzzle pieces fit

Two cold-water checks

Cold water 1: Can "trusted distribution" really land?

  • ⚠️ Sounds great on stage
  • ⚠️ How do you quantify AI-answer-layer influence?
  • ⚠️ What does the customer actually get?
  • ⚠️ This "outcome forecast" system hasn't been market-tested yet

Worth watching.

Cold water 2: The copycats are coming

  • ⚠️ When a lane gets hot, other media will launch similar offerings fast
  • ⚠️ When that happens, the differentiator is real substance: corpus quality, technical base, alliance scale

Not launch-event polish.

One-line summary

For the first time, mainstream media is seriously answering the question "why do I deserve to exist in the AI era?" — the answer isn't to fight AI, but to become the credible source inside AI's answers.

The direction is right; the road is long.

Implications for Hany's four businesses

1. Consumer electronics (smart speakers / AI terminals)

  • ✅ AI answer layer = new traffic entry
  • ✅ Content authority = the new moat
  • ✅ Cross-border e-commerce needs to think about GEO

2. AI compute

  • ✅ Clean corpora = hard demand from AI companies
  • ✅ Authoritative sources = scarce resource
  • ✅ Compute customers now extend beyond model makers to media

3. Cross-border e-commerce

  • ✅ Foreign media alliances = cross-border GEO
  • ✅ Lesser-language authoritative sources = cross-border AI answers

4. Investing in young founders

  • ✅ Zhipu / model makers + media = investment thesis
  • ✅ Source quality = moat for AI companies
  • ✅ GEO service lane = new entrepreneurial opportunity
