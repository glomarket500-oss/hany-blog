《沙丘咖啡、中午河粉、福田電子批發市場嘅「冇人」—— 9月20日深圳三件事》

鄭哥 Hany · 2026 年 9 月 22 日 · 福田

一、今日深圳頭條：李運當選深圳市市長

今日（9 月 20 日）下午 3 時，深圳市第七屆人大第八次會議選舉李運為深圳市人民政府市長。400 多名市人大代表喺市民中心投票，當選後佢進行咗莊嚴嘅憲法宣誓。

佢嘅承諾好實在：

  • ✅ 強生產、擴投資、促消費、穩外貿
  • ✅ 綜合改革試點 + 營商環境
  • ✅ APEC 會議籌辦（深圳國際化）
  • ✅ 產業科技創新中心

今日嘅深圳，迎來新市長、新節奏。

二、田貝建材市場 + 中午河粉 + 沙丘咖啡——一個人嘅小確幸

今日中午，我喺福田做完嘢，行過田貝建材市場附近嘅南園路，揾咗間小店食咗碗河粉。

河粉好食、街坊味、人情味都喺度。

然後我行去咗沙丘咖啡——一間好乾淨嘅精品咖啡店，淺薄荷綠嘅陶瓷杯，金棕色嘅 espresso crema，木托盤、紙巾上印住橙色咖啡豆 logo，寫住「沙丘咖啡」四個字。

坐低，飲一杯，等朋友。

呢個就係深圳嘅慢——一個做電子消費品 26 年嘅外貿老炮兒，喺一個下午，揀咗河粉 + 咖啡 + 等朋友。

三、福田電子批發市場：「冇人」呢個行業

而家講下電子批發市場嘅一個觀察。

田貝雖然以建材五金為主，但華強北、賽格、新亞洲、中航尚君呢啲地方，曾經係中國電子消費品批發嘅心臟。

今日嘅現狀：

現象描述
客流減少實體批發人流明顯下降
B2B 電商1688 + 國際站 + 阿里國際搶走大量訂單
小批量定製客戶要求小單快返，傳統批發利潤薄
直播帶貨一啲檔口轉型直播，部分成功部分失敗
跨境電商一啲老闆出海去東南亞、印度、非洲搵新市場
「冇人」呢個行業——呢句唔係完全真，但反映咗幾個事實： 
過去 20 年嘅電子批發市場
   ↓
今日嘅三大變化
   ↓
1. 信息透明（電商）
2. 客戶分散（全球）
3. 價格戰激烈（毛利薄）
   ↓
新一代嘅電子人，已經喺呢個過程中
搵到自己嘅位置
真正嘅「冇人」，唔係個行業冇人，而係用舊方法做嘢嘅人越嚟越少。

四、仲有一單新聞：福田警方打掉虛假投資詐騙

今日（9 月 20 日）上午，深圳福田警方破獲咗一個虛假境外投資詐騙案，拘捕 26 人，涉案金額過千萬元。

呢啲騙徒用離岸牌照包裝、搭建假嘅外盤交易平台，後台可隨意改行情。投資者以為接駁到國際交易，其實資金完全冇出過境。

對 70 後嘅提醒：

  • ✅ 投資要先查牌照真偽
  • ✅ 資金要確認真實接駁到交易所
  • ✅ 警惕「高回報 + 離岸」嘅組合

五、一個人嘅深圳下午

今日嘅深圳：

  • ✅ 李運當選市長（新節奏）
  • ✅ 田貝 + 沙丘咖啡（一個人嘅小確幸）
  • ✅ 電子批發市場轉型（行業觀察）
  • ✅ 福田警方打假（社會面）

我坐喺沙丘咖啡，等朋友，飲緊 espresso，寫緊呢篇嘢。

呢個就係深圳——
節奏快、行業變、人情暖、新聞多。

"Shaqu Coffee, Noon Pho, and the 'No One' of Futian's Electronics Market — Three Shenzhen Things on September 20"

Hany Zheng Ge · September 22, 2026 · Futian

1. Today's Shenzhen Headline: Li Yun Elected Mayor

Today (Sept 20) at 3 PM, the 7th Shenzhen People's Congress, 8th Session elected Li Yun as Mayor. 400+ delegates voted at Civic Center; he took the constitutional oath.

His commitments:

  • ✅ Strengthen production, expand investment, boost consumption, stabilize trade
  • ✅ Comprehensive reform pilot + business environment
  • ✅ APEC meeting prep (Shenzhen internationalization)
  • ✅ Industrial tech innovation hub

Today's Shenzhen has a new mayor and a new tempo.

2. Tianbei Hardware Market + Noon Pho + Shaqu Cafe — A Solo Small Joy

At noon, after finishing work in Futian, I passed by Nanyuan Road near Tianbei Hardware Market and found a small shop for a bowl of pho.

The noodles were good, full of neighborhood warmth.

Then I walked to Shaqu Cafe — a clean specialty coffee shop. A pale-mint ceramic cup, a golden espresso crema, a wooden tray, a napkin stamped with an orange coffee-bean logo reading "沙丘咖啡".

Sat down, had a cup, waited for a friend.

This is Shenzhen's slow — a 26-year consumer-electronics veteran choosing, on one afternoon, pho + coffee + waiting.

3. Futian Electronics Wholesale Market: The "No One" Industry

Now an observation on the electronics wholesale market.

Tianbei is mainly construction hardware, but Huaqiangbei, Saige, Xinyazhou, and Zhonghang Shangjun were once the heart of China's consumer-electronics wholesale.

Today's reality:

PhenomenonDescription
Foot traffic downPhysical wholesale foot traffic has visibly declined
B2B e-commerce1688 + Alibaba International have taken large order share
Small-batch customCustomers want small orders and fast turnaround; margins thin
LivestreamSome stalls pivoted to livestream; mixed results
Cross-borderSome owners went overseas — SE Asia, India, Africa — for new markets
The "no one" label isn't entirely true, but it reflects several facts: 
20 years of electronics wholesale markets
   ↓
Three big changes today
   ↓
1. Information transparency (e-commerce)
2. Customer dispersion (global)
3. Price war (thin margins)
   ↓
A new generation of electronics people
have found their place in this transition
The real "no one" isn't that the industry is empty — it's that fewer people are still doing it the old way.

4. Another Headline: Futian Police Bust Fake Investment Scam

This morning (Sept 20), Shenzhen Futian police busted a fake offshore investment scam — 26 arrests, over 10 million RMB involved.

The fraudsters packaged themselves with offshore licenses and built fake trading platforms with mutable back-end data. Investors thought they were connecting to international exchanges, but the money never left China.

A reminder for Gen-X investors:

  • ✅ Verify the license's authenticity
  • ✅ Confirm funds actually connect to the exchange
  • ✅ Beware the "high return + offshore" combo

5. One Person's Shenzhen Afternoon

Today's Shenzhen:

  • ✅ Li Yun elected mayor (new tempo)
  • ✅ Tianbei + Shaqu Cafe (a solo small joy)
  • ✅ Electronics wholesale market转型 (industry observation)
  • ✅ Futian police bust scam (social scene)

I'm sitting at Shaqu Cafe, waiting for a friend, drinking an espresso, writing this.

This is Shenzhen —
fast tempo, transforming industries, warm people, heavy news.
