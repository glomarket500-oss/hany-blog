《2005年，5000蚊買咗個專利，我留咗一手》The 5000 HKD Patent That Changed Everything

2026年9月4日 09:12:52Sep 4, 2026 09:12:52 產品創新Innovation 創業故事Startup Story 2005年2005

個客拎住件樣板過嚟，話：「保證俾你5000蚊，幫我把呢個概念做出來？」A client came in with a sample in his hand: “I guarantee you 5,000 dollars. Turn this concept into a product for me?”

我屌，5000蚊？開個模幾錢你知唔知？ 天方夜譚。佢個字仲要細到乜咁。Bloody hell. 5,000 dollars? Do you know what a mould costs? Pure fantasy. And the deposit he was offering was pitifully small.

跟住佢飲茶，我靜咗。隔離有把聲：「老婆，呢個好喎。」Then we went for tea and I sat there saying nothing. From the next table, a voice: “Hey honey, this one's good.”

就係呢一句，我諗深一層——又真係得得哋㖞。That one sentence made me think harder — actually, it might just work.

我同佢講：「得，5000蚊，你個專利賣俾我。嘢你留低，我幫你落地。」I told him: “Fine. 5,000 dollars. Sell me the patent. Leave the thing with me and I'll take it to production.”

但我做生意嘅，點會唔留後路？But I'm a businessman — of course I keep a way out.

我知呢個外國人實會即刻簽，我就同佢簽。錢收咗，但我話：「我先做500個樣板I knew the foreigner would sign right away, so I signed with him. I took the money, but I said: “I'll do 500 samples first

俾你，唔備咁多貨。」表麵係試水溫，實際我專登搞砸呢張單。for you. I won't stock any more than that.” On the surface I was testing the water. In fact I was deliberately botching this order.

因為我自己用佢入麵嘅技術，研究出另一款。我怕佢告我，所以要改到唔同。Because I took the technology inside his sample and developed a different model of my own. I was afraid he would sue me, so I had to change it enough to look nothing like his.

結果？嗰5000蚊買嘅唔止係專利，係成個行業嘅入場券。The result? That 5,000 dollars bought more than a patent. It bought a ticket into the whole industry.

鄭哥補充Hany's Addendum

上麵呢段係當年嘅真事，但只係冰山一角。Everything above really happened, but it is only the tip of the iceberg.

其實個外國客畀我嘅「設計」，根本做唔出嚟。屏幕太細、電池太重、按鍵又密又細，戴喺手度好似綁咗塊磚。如果我真係照佢要求做，呢單嘢一定死。The truth is, the “design” the foreign client handed me could not be built at all. The screen was too small, the battery too heavy, the keys too cramped and tiny. Wearing it on your wrist felt like having a brick strapped on. If I had really built it to his spec, the whole thing was dead.

所以我做咗三件事：So I did three things:

    • 改設計 — 推翻佢成個外殼，屏幕大一倍、按鍵好撳、電池撐足兩日Redesigned it — threw out his entire shell, doubled the screen, made the keys easy to press, and got the battery to last two full days
    • 改型號 — 外觀、結構、電路板佈局全部唔同，佢告我都告唔入Changed the model — different appearance, different structure, different circuit-board layout. He could sue me all he liked; it would never stick
    • 改市場 — 佢想做幾百個試水，我直接聯係歐美客，一次過出貨五千隻Changed the market — he wanted a few hundred units to test the water; I went straight to buyers in Europe and America and shipped five thousand in one go
    ，我直接聯係歐美客，一次過出貨五千隻

嗰500個樣板我做得麻麻地，交完俾佢之後就冇再聯絡。Those 500 samples I made were nothing special. After I handed them over, we never spoke again.

但私底下我自己做嘅版本，已經出咗三代。But privately, my own version had already gone through three generations.

我做嘅嗰隻手錶手機，比 Apple Watch 早咗九年。The watch-phone I built came nine years before the Apple Watch.

好多人以為做生意要讀好多書、識好多技術。我同你講，唔係。我當年唔識模具設計，唔識電路工程，但我識得兩樣嘢：識得問，識得改。A lot of people think doing business means reading a pile of books and knowing a pile of technology. Let me tell you: no. Back then I knew nothing about mould design and nothing about circuit engineering. But I knew two things: how to ask, and how to change.

隔籬枱嗰對夫婦講「呢個好喎」，俾咗我信心——呢個市場真係存在。That couple at the next table saying “this one's good” gave me confidence — this market really did exist.

5000蚊買個概念，值；但買完之後點用，先係真功夫。5,000 dollars for a concept is worth it; but what you do with it after you buy it — that is the real skill.
