《Token中轉站：AI時代嘅「水貨檔」，月流水千萬背後係咩價錢》

【鄭哥點評】 欄目：鄭哥博客點評

Token Relay Station: The AI Era's "Grey Market" — What's Behind the Millions in Monthly Flow?

鄭哥 Hany · 2026 年 9 月 25 日 · 福田

引子：一單舊聞，睇到我背脊涼

今日朝早，有朋友碌到一單舊聞掟畀我：《月流水上千萬元，Token中轉站係一門點樣嘅生意？》。

話係舊聞，其實先係今年6月《21世紀經濟報道》同《界面新聞》做嘅調查，9月再俾人翻出嚟。點解翻熱？因為上個月Anthropic鎖區，Fable 5、Mythos 5唔畀境外用，一夜之間大把中轉站斷貨，成個圈先發現，原來自己一直靠人哋水喉食飯。

我睇完，第一反應唔係驚訝，係熟口熟面。呢單生意，我哋做電子貿易嘅，三十年前就見過——不過當年叫「水貨檔」，而家叫「中轉站」。

第一幕：華強北飲茶，後生仔同我講「唔使買官方」

上星期喺華強北，一個做開發嘅後生仔阿源請我飲茶。佢話，鄭哥，你仲買緊官方API？貴到咁，不如我介紹個中轉站畀你，Claude Opus官方170蚊一百萬Token，佢哋賣78蚊，細站仲平到2折3折，最平嗰間2蚊，好似免費咁。

我問佢，咁平，人哋賺咩？阿源笑，批發囉。中轉站批量買Coding Plan，攤返成本先係0.2蚊一萬Token，官方賣1蚊，佢賣0.5蚊，毛利率六成。一個200美金月租嘅Claude Max賬號，拆開賣畀十幾廿個人，收到二三千美金，高峰去到四五千，25倍槓桿。

我話，咁唔係同當年拆原裝機賣配件一樣？佢話，係呀鄭哥，邊度有牆，邊度就有販子。海外大模型唔畀大陸直接用，價格又貴，販子自然出嚟。

嗰晚返到屋企我就諗，呢句「邊度有牆邊度就有販子」，真係千古不變。

第二幕：三檔生意，越賺錢越灰色

報道寫得好白，中轉站分三檔。

第一檔係正規軍，好似美國OpenRouter咁，大批量問官方攞折扣，再拆開零售。利薄，但乾淨，長做長有。

第二檔係混亂地帶，亦係而家最多人做嗰檔。手法五花八門：批量註冊初創公司呃免費額度、買多份Coding Plan砌號池、逆向Windsurf、Cursor嗰啲內置調用，當喺公共水管上駁條私家喉。正如個站長自己講，「就好似偷水偷電咁」。按官方價2到5折賣，靠就係個「灰」字。

第三檔係無底線，賣假貨。話賣Claude，後台跑緊廉價開源模型。有測試話45%中轉平台存在「身份不匹配」。仲有捲款跑路嗰啲，同行都睇唔起。

我做咗26年外貿，一眼睇得出：三檔生意，對應三種人。第一檔係做長久嘅，第二檔係賭快錢嘅，第三檔係呃一鑊就走嘅。而家個市，恰恰係第二檔最多人，因為Claude實在太貴，一輪對話燒五六毫，一般人頂唔順，平嘢自然有人吼。

第三幕：最毒唔係貴，係「一魚兩吃」

阿源同我講，有個站長朋友阿K，一個人加兩台服務器，一個月充值流水過百萬。技術門檻低到咩地步？開源有現成方案，New API、sub2 API，買台服務器，同AI講聲幫我部署，唔識技術都開得檔。發個帖，24小時塞爆11個兩百人群。收88蚊到199蚊「賦能課」，再賣幾千上萬企業部署陪跑，賣鏟先係最暴利。

但我就同阿源講，後生仔，你睇到人哋賺，睇唔睇到人哋點賺？報道入面有句話好準：「對中轉站嚟講，底線係唔摻假、唔賣數據，但兩樣都好難。唔使用成本就有人送錢，好少人頂得住。」

咩叫一魚兩吃？一面用假模型呃你Token錢，一面將你嘅對話、代碼、商業資料打包，0.1蚊一次賣畀數據公司。有站長親口認，成日有人問買唔買用戶日誌，點名要Claude 4.6以上、30輪以上對話。你慳返嗰幾十蚊，賠出去嘅可能係成盤生意。代碼入面俾人插木馬，偷你雲憑證、加密資產，呢啲唔係嚇人，係已經發生緊。

我同阿源講，你寫代碼嗰啲核心密碼，千祈唔好經中轉站。佢話知，重要嘢一律行公司自建嘅國產模型。我話，呢個先係老江湖。

第四幕：風，已經轉咗

點解話呢門生意短命？三個信號，一個比一個硬。

第一，官方落閘。Anthropic今年4月 cut咗第三方harness，6月12日直接鎖區，最勁嗰兩款模型唔畀境外用。中轉站九成需求衝住Claude嚟，無咗Claude，成盤生意塌一半。

第二，差佬出手。2026年5月，上海已經有人因為非法逆向爬取、倒賣低價接口，被刑拘37日。孫宇晨5月1日高調出B.AI，話區塊鏈登錄、匿名支付、全網最平，畀人質疑繞過身份鏈路；同日傅盛出EasyRouter，好快改口叫「企業級模型網關」，聲明唔做大陸服務。兩個大佬急住洗白，你話呢行水有幾深。

第三，國產反攻。今年一季度OpenRouter榜單，DeepSeek、月之暗面、MiniMax、智譜、千問輪住坐頭幾位。國產模型又平又好，仲反向出口去海外。夏達嗰班老站長已經轉口風：暴利風口已過，以後係國產Token出海、細水長流。

鄭哥三句掰扯

第一句：販子生意永遠有人做，但長久嘅錢一定係乾淨嘅錢。 當年山寨機、串貨水貨，全部係需求爆發同規則滯後之間嘅罅隙生意。罅隙永遠有，但靠罅隙發大財嘅，遲早要還。

第二句：信息差係會到期嘅。 今日你賺人哋唔識、賺人哋上唔到官方，聽日官方一鎖區、一降價，你就無嘢賺。真正嘅護城河唔係貨源灰唔灰，係服務穩唔穩、數據守唔守得住。

第三句：我哋賣API嘅，要行正路。 我自己都係賣算力、賣API嘅人，呢篇嘢某程度上係寫畀自己。正規批量採購、企業私有部署、國產模型出海，三條路，利薄但瞓得着。靠逆向同黑卡撐場面，風口一過，第一個死。

一句話總結

Token中轉站係AI算力爆發同規則滯後之間催生嘅灰色套利，暴利靠信息差同資金沉澱撐起，但代價係用戶數據裸奔同經營者法律風險。販子永遠有，但想做長久，就要做乾淨。

Token Relay Station: The AI Era's "Grey Market" — What's Behind the Millions in Monthly Flow?

Hany Zheng Ge · September 25, 2026 · Futian

1. Prelude: An Old News Item That Sent a Chill Down My Spine

This morning, a friend tossed an old news item my way: "Monthly flow over ten million — what kind of business is a token relay station?"

Though called "old news," it was actually from a June investigation by 21st Economic Report and Jiemian News, resurfaced in September. Why the sudden buzz? Because last month Anthropic locked down regions — Fable 5 and Mythos 5 no longer available to overseas users. Overnight, countless token relay stations ran dry, and the whole circle realized: we've been living off other people's water pipes.

Reading it, my first reaction wasn't surprise — it was familiarity. This business? I've seen it before in thirty years of electronics trading — except back then it was called "grey market," now it's "token relay station."

2. First Act: Tea at Huaqiangbei, a Young Tells Me "Don't Buy Official"

Last week at Huaqiangbei, a young developer named A-Yuan invited me for tea. He said, "Uncle Zheng, you're still buying the official API? It's so expensive — let me introduce you to a token relay station. Claude Opus official is 170 bucks per million tokens; they sell it for 78, small stalls go as low as 20-30% of official price, the cheapest is just 2 bucks — almost free."

I asked, "If it's that cheap, how do they make a profit?" A-Yuan laughed, "Wholesale, of course." Token relay stations buy Coding Plans in bulk, amortizing the cost to 0.2 bucks per 10K tokens, while the official price is 1 buck; they sell at 0.5 bucks, a 60% gross margin. A $200/month Claude Max account gets split among a dozen or twenty users, pulling in two to three thousand bucks, peaking at four to five thousand — 25x leverage.

I said, "Isn't this just like the old days of tearing apart genuine devices to sell spare parts?" He replied, "Exactly, Uncle Zheng — where there's a wall, there's a smuggler. Overseas AI models aren't directly available in mainland China, and they're pricey, so smugglers naturally appear."

That night, lying in bed, I reflected: "Where there's a wall, there's a smuggler" — truly timeless.

3. Second Act: Three Tiers — the More You Earn, the Greyer It Gets

The report lays it out clearly: token relay stations come in three tiers.

The first tier is the legitimate players — think US OpenRouter — buying in bulk for official discounts, then reselling retail. Thin margins, but clean, sustainable long-term.

The second tier is the grey zone — and this is where most people operate today. Tactics are varied: registering shell companies to grab free credits, buying multiple Coding Plans to build pools, reverse-engineering Windsurf or Cursor's internal calls, tapping into public water pipes with private lines. As one station chief put it, "It's just like stealing water or stealing electricity." Selling at 20%-50% of official price, the profit hinges on the "grey" factor.

The third tier is the bottomless pit — selling fake goods. They claim to offer Claude, but the backend runs cheap open-source models. Tests show 45% of relay stations suffer "identity mismatch." There are also exit scams, and peers look down on them.

Having spent 26 years in foreign trade, I can instantly see: the three tiers correspond to three types of people. The first tier are in it for the long haul; the second are gambling on quick money; the third are in and out in one pot. Today's market? The second tier dominates — because Claude is simply too expensive. A single conversation burns five to six cents; ordinary users can't keep up, so cheaper alternatives naturally attract a crowd.

4. Third Act: The Greatest Harm Isn't the Price — It's "Killing Two Birds with One Stone"

A-Yuan told me about his friend A-K, one guy with two servers, charging over a million in monthly flow. How low is the technical bar? Open-source solutions like New API and sub2 API exist — buy a server, tell the AI to help deploy it, and even non-technical folks can get it running. Post an ad, and within 24 hours, eleven 200-person groups are flooded. They sell "empowerment courses" for 88 to 199 bucks, then sell deployment support to thousands of enterprises — selling the shovel is where the real profits lie.

But I told A-Yuan, "Young man, you see them making money, but do you see how they make it?" The report has a telling line: "For token relay stations, the baseline is no adulteration, no selling of data — but both are hard to uphold. If you don't count costs, someone will send you money — but few can stomach it."

What is "killing two birds with one stone"? One face uses a fake model to suck your token money; the other face packages your chats, code, and business data, selling it for 0.1 bucks per pop to data companies. A station chief openly admitted: people daily ask to buy user logs, specifically wanting Claude 4.6+ with 30+ rounds of conversation. You save a few dozen bucks, but the loss could be your entire business. Codebases get planted with trojans to steal your cloud credentials and encrypted assets — these aren't hypotheticals; they're happening now.

I told A-Yuan, "When you write code, guard those core passwords with your life — never let them pass through a token relay station." He replied, "I know — important stuff always runs on self-built domestic models." I said, "That's old-gangster wisdom."

5. Fourth Act: The Wind Has Already Shifted

Why is this business doomed? Three signals, each stronger than the last.

First, the authorities clamp down. Anthropic cut third-party harness in April, then on June 12th locked down regions entirely — the two strongest models no longer serve overseas users. Since token relay stations rely on Claude for about 90% of demand, losing it cuts the business in half.

Second, the heavies step in. In May 2026, Shanghai detained someone for three-odd weeks for illegally scraping and reselling low-price interfaces. On May 1st, Sun Yuchen launched B.AI, touting blockchain login and anonymous payments as "the flattest net," inviting questions about sidestepping identity chains; the same day, Fu Sheng rolled out EasyRouter, quickly renamed to "enterprise-grade model gateway," declaring no mainland services. Both big players rushed to white-wash — you tell me how deep this water really is.

Third, the domestic counter-offensive. In Q1's OpenRouter ranking, DeepSeek, Moon's Dark Side, MiniMax, Zhipu, and Qianwen sit at the top. Domestic models are both cheap and capable, and they're already exporting overseas. The old-station veterans led by Xia Da have already shifted tone: "The profiteering window is over; from now on, it's domestic token exports and slow, steady flow."

Zheng Ge's Three Points

First: Smuggler businesses will always exist, but lasting wealth must be clean money. Back then, shanzhai phones, smuggled goods, and water-grey wares were all born from explosive demand and lagging regulation. The gaps will always be there, but getting rich off them? Sooner or later, you'll have to pay it back.

Second: Information gaps expire. Today you profit from others' ignorance or inability to reach the official channel; tomorrow, the official locks the door or drops the price — and you're left with nothing. The true moat isn't whether your supplies are grey or clean; it's whether your service is steady and your data is guarded.

Third: As API sellers, we must walk the straight path. I myself sell compute and sell APIs — this piece is, in part, written for myself. Legitimate bulk procurement, private enterprise deployment, and domestic-model overseas export — three roads. Margins are thin, but you can sleep soundly. Relying on reverse engineering and black cards to hold the line? When the hype passes, the first to die is whoever leaned on those tricks.

One-Line Summary

Token relay stations are the grey-market arbitrage spawned by the explosion of AI compute power and lagging regulation — profits rests on information asymmetry and capital sedimentation, but the cost is exposed user data and legal risk for operators. Smugglers will always be around, but if you want to last, you must run clean.
